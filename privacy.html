A little privacy, too.

Slouch uses your front camera only when you choose camera controls. Face processing happens on your device. Slouch does not upload or record camera video, images, face meshes, or biometric templates.

The game temporarily uses head orientation and position relative to your camera to control movement. Session summaries, settings, earned items, and progress are stored on this device. Summaries include estimated movement ranges, moving time, and completed activities. They are not medical measurements or a diagnosis.

You can use touch or keyboard without the camera. Camera access stops when you end a session or leave the app. You can revoke access in your device or browser settings.

Sharing a session card is optional and uses your device's share controls. Progress exports contain your saved activity and settings; only share them with people you intend to receive them. Reminders are optional and can be disabled in Settings. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

GitHub Pages hosts the website and receives ordinary web requests when you download it. The packaged iOS app bundles its starting content. No account is required. “Wipe all data” clears your current local progress; exported files and operating-system backups are controlled separately.

Questions or deletion help: Slouch project support.
